Parrot Breeder - Parrot Maintenance
Scientifically formulated as completely balanced extruded lentil shaped kibble, suitable for parrots and parakeets that use their beak for eating. Fun and light with a particular flavor that encourages feeding. No synthetic preservatives.
The Product – NDF, Natural Digestive Formula
  • Provides superb natural nutrition, more balanced than seed-only diets
  • Specially formulated to support efficient digestion and nourishment
  • Enriched with natural additives
  • Stimulates positive intestinal bacteria thus assisting natural digestion
  • Contains aromatics and phytogenics (natural growth promoters derived from herbs, spices or other plants)
  • Contains concentrates from a variety of herbal plants, tannins from oak tree bark and natural oils that improve food absorption
  • Contains rosemary, grapefruit, marigold and grape-skin extracts that form natural antioxidants
  • No food colouring or artificial aromatic and taste additives
  • No use of animal protein
NDF supplies a high percentage of the proteins, fats, amino acids, vitamins and calcium especially necessary for the mating period as well as for the parrots’ healthy day to day living. Constituting at least 60-70% of its daily nutrition, this balanced and precise mix of ingredients has been meticulously tested and chosen in order to provide the best and healthiest solution for your parrot’s natural requirements.
